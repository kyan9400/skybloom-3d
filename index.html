01 Ground

We start with the soil, not the skyline.

Before a single pile is driven we map water, wind and the species already living on the plot. The masterplan bends around them.

02 Rise

Glass where light matters. Timber everywhere else.

Mass-timber cores and triple-glazed skins go up in weeks, not years — prefabricated within 80 km of the site.

03 Bloom

Every floor is a planting plan.

Terraces, facade troughs and roof meadows are engineered into the structure from day one, irrigated by rain the building catches itself.

04 Dusk

A district that keeps living after hours.

When the light goes, the gardens keep cooling the streets and the windows come on one by one. This is what we hand over: a neighbourhood, already alive.

Principles

How we build.

Four rules we do not negotiate on, written into every contract we sign.

  1. 40%

    Plant first

    Planting is drawn before floor plans. Forty per cent of every plot stays open soil.

  2. 90%

    Catch every drop

    Roof meadows and cisterns hold nine-tenths of rainfall on site and feed the facade gardens.

  3. −62%

    Build light

    Mass timber stores carbon instead of emitting it: embodied CO₂ is down 62% against concrete.

  4. 80 km

    Stay nearby

    Eighty per cent of components are made within 80 km of the site, by people who live there.

Process

From plot to neighbourhood.

Five steps, roughly three years, and a decade of looking after what we planted.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Six months of site ecology surveys and resident workshops before any drawing.

  2. 02

    Draw

    A masterplan drawn around existing trees, water flows and prevailing wind.

  3. 03

    Prefabricate

    Timber cassettes and planters built off-site, within 80 km.

  4. 04

    Rise

    Structure and skin in under twenty weeks per tower, with almost no site waste.

  5. 05

    Tend

    We stay on as gardeners for ten years, then hand the tools to residents.
